During this session, I completed three hands-on activities related to web browsers. These activities helped me better understand how browsers work, how they can be customized, and how browser extensions can improve my Internet experience. I also learned that choosing the right browser and configuring its settings can make browsing more efficient, secure, and convenient. The first activity I completed was installing and comparing different web browsers. I installed and explored Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox and compared their interfaces, performance, features, and ease of use. I noticed that both browsers provide common features such as tabs, bookmarks, history, private browsing, and customizable settings, but their layouts and available options are somewhat different. This activity helped me understand that there is not necessarily one browser that is best for everyone. Personally, I can apply what I learned by choosing the browser that works best for the tasks I perform regularly. I can also use multiple browsers when necessary, such as using one browser for everyday activities and another for testing websites or troubleshooting compatibility issues. The second activity I completed was configuring browser settings. I explored the browser's settings and changed options related to the homepage, privacy, cookies, security, and appearance. I learned that browser settings can have an important effect on both convenience and online privacy. For example, controlling cookies and reviewing privacy settings can help me make more informed decisions about how websites collect and use information. I also learned that customizing the browser's appearance and behavior can make it easier to use. I can apply this knowledge personally by regularly reviewing my browser settings, keeping security features enabled, and adjusting settings when my browsing needs change. The third activity I completed was reviewing and managing browser extensions. I examined the extensions available in my browser and learned how extensions can add useful features such as password management, advertisement blocking, grammar checking, and improved productivity. I also learned that extensions should be selected carefully because some extensions may request access to information about websites I visit. I can apply this knowledge by only installing extensions from trustworthy sources, reviewing the permissions they request, and disabling or removing extensions that I no longer need. This activity showed me that browser extensions can be useful tools when they are managed responsibly. Overall, these activities gave me practical experience with web browsers and helped me understand how browser selection, settings, and extensions can affect my Internet experience. I can use these skills for school, work, and everyday online activities.
